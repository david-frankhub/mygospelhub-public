About Us

Music, videos, and gist — one home for the gospel scene.

MyGospelHub is a platform built for gospel music, video, and entertainment news, giving artists, creators, and fans one place to share and discover what's happening in the gospel space.

🎵 Music

Stream and download gospel songs from established and independent artists.

🎬 Video

Watch worship sessions, music videos, and live performances.

📰 Gist

Stay updated with entertainment news from the gospel community.

We started MyGospelHub with a simple belief: gospel content deserves a dedicated home — not scattered across platforms not built for it. Whether it's a brand-new single, a live worship recording, or the latest gist from the scene, MyGospelHub brings it together in one place.

We're just getting started, and we're building this with our community — artists, listeners, and everyone who believes gospel music and entertainment deserve a real spotlight.
